# O-062: Add timeline zoom and timed labels

## Outcome

A visitor can zoom the timeline into a crowded stretch of time and back, at phone size first, and a scene can give a label a stretch of time outside which it does not show.

## Why

The Voyagers review (owner, 2026-11-10): dense early years were hard to read on the timeline, and labels could not be tied to a stretch of time. Issues I-156, I-157.

## Success Conditions

To be detailed when this Objective is planned; the timeline zoom needs an owner design interview (how a visitor zooms, how it shows, phone and desktop) before Tasks.

## Boundaries

**In scope:** the timeline control, the label time range on the scene builder, README, API lock.

**Out of scope:** breaking changes; host-link work (O-061); planet positions (O-063).
