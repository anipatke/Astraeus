## Rework: looping playback (owner, 2026-10-15)

Owner, verbatim: "needs to play in a loop, as disappears when reaches the end". Lifecycle build, test, audit (2026-10-15T20:28:48Z).

- **Cause.** The stream group is bounded by the activity window and `PointsView.sync` draws nothing outside a group's bounds (`#drawn` false, points hidden); the clock never stopped or wrapped at the window's end, so playing past it left the stream hidden. Measured: with `?loop=off`, 3 days past the end the stream's amber pixels in the Focus stream framing fell from 13,592 to 7,893 (the rest is orbit lines).
