# O-101: Group labels the host controls

## Outcome

A host decides which point groups carry their own name label (`addGroup` takes `showLabel`), and places labels of its own (`scene.addLabels`, name final at detailing under API-04): each on a fixed point (`positionKm` with `frame` and `center`) or on the dots of one or more groups, where it sits on the dots (the densest part, or the drawn dot nearest their mean direction from the centre) and follows them as time moves, re-placed at the refill cadence. A tap on a host label selects one group as the object list does, selects several groups together (camera on their combined dots, one card), or opens a panel; with nothing named it is text only. Host labels join the one overlap and priority layout with their own `labelPriority`, and taps and drags on them follow the #33 rules (belt #36).

## Why

The belt draws populations as several brightness groups and today gets one label per split (21 and 33 labels in its two views, of which 10 and 14 name something a visitor points at), and a ring round the Sun is labelled at the Sun. It blocks the belt's O-007. G-027 finding 2 lists what the API already allows.

## Success Conditions

Detailed 2026-11-24 (planning session plan-o101-20261124) from the owner's G-027 Decisions 1 to 5 and the belt brief.

- **Groups can start unnamed (T-458).** `addGroup` takes `showLabel`, meaning what it means for a body (Decision 1): `false` starts the name hidden, the Labels switch and `?labels=` can show it; the group stays listed, in the camera menu and selectable. A non-boolean throws `bad-group` naming `showLabel`.
- **Names sit on their dots (T-459).** Every group's own name is placed by one rule (Decision 4): the dot nearest the dots' mean direction from their centre, or, for a ring or shell with no clear mean direction, where it crosses the held direction (first the vernal equinox), chosen at each exact fill and blended with the dots between fills; it follows them in time. A ring round the Sun is named on the ring. The camera still focuses and frames the centroid.
- **Host labels (T-460, T-461).** `scene.addLabels([{ id, label, color?, labelPriority?, anchor, selects?, description?, provenance?, knownLimitations? }])`: `anchor` is `{ positionKm, plane, center? }` or `{ groups }`; a label on groups lands within their dots, ring included, by the same rule pooled over the groups, and follows them. Host labels are entries of the one label layout with their own `labelPriority` (default 80). A group named in any label's `anchor.groups` draws no name of its own; the Labels switch and `?labels=` show and hide the host label instead.
- **Taps (T-461, T-462).** `selects: { groups: [one] }` selects that group exactly as the Object list does; `selects: { groups: [several] }` selects them together under the label's id, the camera framing their combined dots and the card being the host's (Decision 2); `selects: { panel }` opens that story or shelf panel; no `selects`: text only. ## Architectural Considerations

- One label layout: host labels are entries in `app/SceneLabels.ts`, as sky labels are, never a second layer; the several-group object's name is its host label (one entry per id).
- One placement rule, `app/groupLabelPoint.ts`, for a group's own name and for host labels on one or several groups; computed at the fill cadence from the fills `app/PointsView.ts` already makes (CPU and graphics-chip modes), not per frame per dot.
- Picking: host labels with `selects` are label-only tap targets in `app/scenePicking.ts`, as a group's name is (O-095); text-only labels are no target.
- The several-group selection is an unlisted object of the scene with a combined camera target (`app/GroupSetTarget.ts`), so it works through `handle.selected`, `setSelected` and `onSelect` like any object, on both embedding paths (API-02); no new handle method or React prop.
- New `SceneTargets` fields are optional, so existing test fixtures stand.
- API-04: the fixed point's frame field is `plane` (the brief said `frame`), its centre `center`, its position `positionKm`.
- Parallel builds: wave 1 is T-458, T-459 and T-460 side by side (disjoint code; shared CHANGELOG bullets and the regenerated API lock, see `independence`); then T-461, T-462 and T-463 in order. O-100's T-457 (`shelf/chart/`) shares no file with any O-101 Task except the CHANGELOG list.
