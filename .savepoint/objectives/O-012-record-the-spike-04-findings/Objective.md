# O-012: Record the Spike 04 findings

## Outcome

`docs/ASTRAEUS_SPIKE_04.md` answers each Definition of Done question in the brief with evidence links, and `.savepoint/Design.md` is reconciled.

## Why

The brief makes the findings, not the demo, the spike's deliverable: a Perseids demo with unproven abstractions is a failed spike.

## Success Conditions

Each of the six Definition of Done questions has an answer backed by named tests, browser evidence or audit rows; remaining limitations and the ground-view gap are stated.

## Architectural Considerations

Findings describe implemented reality; recommendations for later spikes are labelled as such.

## Boundaries

**In scope:** findings note, Design reconciliation.
**Out of scope:** new code.
