## Outcome

After the first public launch (G-005), the planets beyond Jupiter get the Jupiter treatment: their moons as globes with Planetary Explorer's tagged places, on real orbits around real parent planets, shown in system views, on the home-page shelf and in the assets catalogue.

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: "We will do the same for the other planets, but post launch is ok". O-018 and O-019 built the groundwork (the body packages and importer; the measured moon orbits) before the launch; the owner chose on 2026-10-18 to shrink them to that groundwork and to put the remaining moons work in a new post-launch Goal rather than the Jupiter stories or clean-up Goals.

## Success Conditions

Every member Objective is done.

## Boundaries

Moons and planets beyond what the launch ships, built from the O-018 packages, the O-019 orbit records and the Jupiter pattern from O-025. Publishing and deployment are the owner's actions, not this Goal's.
