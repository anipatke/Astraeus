Cause: the stream's exact fill (18,699 members, about 15 ms) runs once per refill span of simulated time; at the new default playback of a week per second the 6 h span passed every frame or two. The span is a setting (look.refillAfterHours); 72 h blends straight lines between fills three days apart, a sag under 0.001 AU on a 1 AU-radius arc. Not the cause: the Sun's halo (?sunglow=0 same), the sky (?sky=off same), labels (all off same), render passes (single-sun same), per-frame React work (a 100 ms interval only; paused script time equals the baseline's).
The software-GL gap that remains with no stream (43 against 60) is raster/command time in the GL process (TaskDuration 24-27 ms against 8-10), not script; not measurable as a real-GPU figure here.
